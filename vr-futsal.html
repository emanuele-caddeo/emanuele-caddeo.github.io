VR Futsal Tactical Training Simulator

A standalone VR prototype that uses body-tracking to simulate futsal match scenarios, enabling players to rehearse positioning, movement, and team tactics in an immersive environment.

Featured image · video · GIF

Overview

This project is a standalone virtual reality simulator designed for futsal tactical training. Using body-tracking technology, the system places players inside an immersive virtual pitch where they can rehearse positioning, movement patterns, and team coordination without physical constraints or the need for a full team to be present.

The simulator targets the challenge of tactical assimilation — helping players internalize complex plays and formations faster than traditional whiteboard sessions or video analysis allow. By embodying the scenarios rather than observing them, players develop spatial intuition for positioning and timing.

Although still in prototype stage, the system is fully functional. It was also developed as the subject of a research project, exploring how immersive technologies can accelerate learning in team sports training contexts.
