Android Smartwatch Applications

Lightweight, responsive Wear OS applications designed around the constraints of small circular screens, quick glance interactions, and sensor integration.

Featured image · video · GIF

Overview

Developing for Wear OS requires a different design mindset compared to standard Android. Screen real estate is minimal, interactions must be instantaneous, and battery life is a critical constraint. These projects focused on building applications that feel at home on a smartwatch — fast to launch, clear to read at a glance, and efficient with system resources.

The work covered a range of interaction patterns typical of wearables: complications, ambient mode, sensor data (heart rate, accelerometer), and connectivity with companion smartphone apps. UI design favored large, legible typography and minimal navigation depth.

Each project was built using Kotlin and the Wear OS SDK, following current Android development practices and Jetpack Compose for Wear where applicable.
