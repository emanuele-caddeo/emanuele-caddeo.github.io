AI Recruiting Interview System

An autonomous AI-powered platform that conducts job interviews via text or voice, with built-in compliance enforcement for fairness and anti-discrimination regulations.

Featured image · video · GIF

Overview

This project was developed as part of a collaborative initiative between the University of Cagliari and a company specializing in applied AI tools. The goal was to design and build an AI-powered recruiting platform capable of conducting job interviews autonomously, without human intervention during the interview itself.

The system supports two interaction modes: text-based chat and fully voice-based conversation, allowing organizations to choose the format best suited to their recruiting pipeline. A core design constraint was regulatory compliance — the interviewer is configured to avoid any questions or evaluation patterns that could violate anti-discrimination laws or fairness standards.

The interviewer behavior is fully configurable: organizations can define the interview style, question bank, evaluation criteria, and pacing through a configuration interface, making the system adaptable to different roles, sectors, and recruiting workflows.
